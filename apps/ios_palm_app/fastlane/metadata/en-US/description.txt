Explore palm lines through modern computer vision and the cultural traditions of Samudrika Shastra. PalmReader combines guided photo capture, editable line overlays, and simple rule-based interpretations for entertainment and personal reflection.

KEY FEATURES:

• Guided Palm Capture
Use the live hand guide and feedback on positioning, lighting, and steadiness to help capture your palm. Capture quality varies with your camera and surroundings.

• Palm Line Overlays
Review suggested Heart, Head, Life, and Fate lines on your photo. Detection can be uncertain; review and adjust the lines before continuing.

• Interactive Line Editor with Magnifying Loupe
Move points, redraw curves, and change line labels. Zoom and use the magnifying loupe to inspect visible creases.

• Cultural Palm Readings
Read simple local interpretations based on the shape, length, and clarity of the lines you review. These are cultural interpretations, not scientifically validated assessments of personality or predictions of future events. Life-line length does not indicate lifespan or health.
