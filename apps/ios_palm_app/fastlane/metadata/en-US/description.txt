Palm Reading Scanner helps you capture a clear palm, map major lines (Life, Heart, Head, Fate), and review a grounded reading — with edits you control.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Guided camera capture so your palm is framed and lit correctly
• On-device line detection for Life, Heart, Head, and Fate
• Edit lines before reading — drag, relabel, and confirm what counts
• Clear reading card based on confirmed geometry, not raw guesses
• Works offline for core scanning on your device

BUILT FOR REAL USE
This is a helper, not an oracle. The app proposes lines; you correct them quickly, then lock them for the reading. Your corrections improve future proposals.

PRIVACY
Camera access is used only to analyze your palm on device for guidance and crease mapping. Review our privacy policy for details.
