Version 1.0.0 - Initial Release

🎉 First release of ASD Gaze Collector — the first FDA-grade at-home autism screening tool on iPhone.

NEW FEATURES:
• 30-second Express and 60-second Comprehensive screening batteries
• GeoPref split-screen, Face Scanning, Joint Attention, and Oculomotor stimulus clips
• 16 continuous digital biomarkers computed on-device at 60 Hz
• M-CHAT-R/F 20-item caregiver survey with instant scoring and Follow-Up flag
• Clinician-ready PDF report + FHIR R4 interoperable data export
• SMART on FHIR OAuth 2.0 PKCE for direct EHR integration (Epic, Cerner)
• CareKit longitudinal tracking: mood diary, medication log, milestones
• ResearchKit consent, encrypted data vault, study sync
• Zero raw video recording — AES-GCM-256 encrypted telemetry only

CLINICAL VALIDATION:
• Biomarkers mapped to Duke SenseToKnow, EarliPoint, GeoPref literature
• IRB Protocol #2026-088, COPPA/HIPAA aligned
• De-identified by default, zero raw video storage

SUPPORTED DEVICES:
• iPhone 12 or later with TrueDepth camera
• iOS 18.0+
