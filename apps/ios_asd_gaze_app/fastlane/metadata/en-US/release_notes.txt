First release of EarlyGaze Observation Capture.

• Short observation sessions that note where a child looks and how their face moves
• Sessions are stored on the device
• Sessions are delivered to your clinic's console once the clinic enrols the device
• Delete everything from Settings

This is not a diagnostic tool. Part of Open Spectrum Tracking.

Requires iOS 18.0 or later. Observation sessions need an iPhone or iPad with Face ID (TrueDepth camera).
