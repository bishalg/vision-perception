Welcome to EarlyGaze Observation Diary.

• Short observation sessions that note where your child looks and how their face moves
• Mood diary, milestones and medication log in one place
• PDF summary you can bring to a pediatrician visit
• Camera frames are never stored or sent anywhere
• Delete all of your data from Settings

This is not a diagnostic tool. Part of Open Spectrum Tracking.

Requires iOS 18.0 or later. Observation sessions need an iPhone or iPad with Face ID (TrueDepth camera).
