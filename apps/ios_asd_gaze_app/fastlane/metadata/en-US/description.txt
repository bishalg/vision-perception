EarlyGaze is an at-home developmental visual attention observation and monitoring tool designed for parents and caregivers to track social engagement patterns in toddlers aged 12–60 months.

Using the iPhone's front-facing TrueDepth camera, EarlyGaze captures real-time 3D gaze direction and facial affect during standardized visual engagement activities (social versus geometric preference, face scanning, and joint attention tasks). Continuous digital engagement metrics are computed entirely on-device — including social visual preference, eye dwell ratio, first-eye orienting latency, and shared facial expressivity.

Key Features:
• Zero Raw Video Storage — camera frames are processed strictly in volatile RAM; no photos or videos of your child are ever stored or transmitted
• 30-second Express & 60-second Standard observation sessions
• Standardized M-CHAT-R/F 20-item caregiver questionnaire with instant review
• Clinician-ready PDF export summarizing visual engagement trends for pediatrician visits
• CareKit longitudinal tracking: milestone tracker, caregiver mood diary, and routine logs
• Standardized health interoperability exports (FHIR R4 format) for pediatric records
• 100% On-Device Processing powered by the Apple Neural Engine (ANE)

Scientific Foundation:
EarlyGaze visual engagement metrics reference published pediatric research, including the GeoPref paradigm (Pierce et al., UC San Diego) and Duke University SenseToKnow visual attention studies.

IMPORTANT MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:
EarlyGaze is an observational developmental monitoring aid and educational screening reference. It is NOT a medical device, does NOT provide a diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) or any medical condition, and does NOT replace professional pediatric or psychiatric evaluation. Always seek the advice of a qualified pediatrician or licensed healthcare professional with any questions regarding your child's developmental health or before making any medical decisions.
