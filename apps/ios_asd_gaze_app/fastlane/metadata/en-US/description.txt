EarlyGaze Observation Capture records a short gaze observation session for your clinic. The child watches short clips while the front TrueDepth camera notes where they look and their facial expressions. Everything is processed on the device, and no photos or video are saved. Nothing is interpreted on the device.

How a session works:
1. Check the camera and calibrate with the operator.
2. The child watches short, child-friendly clips.
3. The app notes where the child looks and how their face moves.
4. The session is stored on the device, or delivered to your clinic once it is enrolled.

Your clinic reviews each session:
Sessions stay on the device until your clinic enrols it with a clinic code. Once enrolled, sessions are delivered to the clinic's console, where clinicians review them on their own platform. The app itself shows no result.

Privacy:
• Camera frames are processed in memory and are never saved or sent
• Sessions are encrypted on the device and excluded from backups
• You can delete everything from Settings

Device requirements:
Requires iOS 18 or later and an iPhone or iPad with Face ID (TrueDepth camera). On other devices the app can be used for settings only.

This is not a diagnostic tool.
It does not measure, screen for or diagnose any condition; your clinic reviews each session. EarlyGaze does not diagnose any condition and is not a medical device.
