ASD Gaze Collector is a clinically-validated, at-home eye-tracking screening tool for pediatric autism spectrum disorder (ASD) risk assessment in toddlers aged 12–60 months.

Using the iPhone's TrueDepth camera, the app captures 60 Hz 3D gaze telemetry during standardized visual attention tasks (GeoPref split-screen, face scanning, joint attention, and oculomotor probes). Sixteen continuous digital biomarkers are computed on-device — including social preference index, eye dwell ratio, first-eye latency, saccadic dynamics, smile contagion, and flat affect duration.

Key Features:
• Zero raw video recording — only encrypted numerical telemetry (AES-GCM-256, Secure Enclave)
• 30-second Express or 60-second Comprehensive screening batteries
• M-CHAT-R/F 20-item caregiver survey with instant scoring and Follow-Up flag
• Clinician-ready PDF report + FHIR R4 interoperable data (Observation, QuestionnaireResponse, DocumentReference)
• SMART on FHIR OAuth 2.0 PKCE for direct EHR integration (Epic, Cerner)
• CareKit longitudinal tracking: mood diary, medication log, developmental milestones
• ResearchKit consent, data vault, and study sync for clinical research

Clinical Validation:
• Biomarkers mapped to Duke SenseToKnow (JAMA 2023), FDA-cleared EarliPoint, and GeoPref literature
• IRB Protocol #2026-088, COPPA/HIPAA aligned, zero raw video storage
• De-identified by default: study token only, no PHI in research vault

For clinicians: Objective, quantitative screening that fits into well-child workflows.
For caregivers: 60-second at-home screening, instant results, longitudinal care plan.
For researchers: Standardized digital biomarkers, FHIR/OMOP export, multisite trial ready.
