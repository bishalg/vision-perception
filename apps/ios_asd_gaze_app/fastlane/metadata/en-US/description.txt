EarlyGaze Observation Diary helps parents and caregivers keep a private record of how a young child engages with the world, alongside everyday notes.

Part of Open Spectrum Tracking: parents' daily observations become structured, shareable records, and you decide whether to share them with your child's clinician.

What you can do:
• Watch short, child-friendly videos while the front TrueDepth camera notes where your child looks and how their face moves, as simple engagement measures
• Keep a mood diary, milestone list and medication log in one place
• Review each session as an easy-to-read engagement summary
• Export a PDF summary to bring to a pediatrician visit
• Share standard health records (FHIR R4 format) only when you choose

Privacy first:
• Camera frames are processed in memory on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere
• Session data stays on your device, encrypted and excluded from backups
• You can delete everything from Settings

Requires iOS 18 or later. Observation sessions need an iPhone or iPad with Face ID (TrueDepth front camera); on other devices, including Apple Vision Pro, the diary, reminders and notes still work.

This is not a diagnostic tool.
EarlyGaze Observation Diary does not diagnose any condition, is not a medical device, and does not replace advice from a qualified pediatrician or other licensed professional. Talk to your child's doctor about any concerns about their development.
