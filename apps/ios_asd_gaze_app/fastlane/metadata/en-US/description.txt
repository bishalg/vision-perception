EarlyGaze is a non-invasive, objective digital screening aid designed to help parents, pediatricians, and clinical researchers monitor early social visual development and attentional patterns in toddlers aged 12 to 48 months.

Using the TrueDepth sensor on your iPhone or iPad, EarlyGaze tracks eye gaze fixations and facial affect in real time while your child watches engaging, pediatric-calibrated video clips—transforming complex laboratory eye-tracking into a gentle 60-second home screening experience.

KEY FEATURES:

• Clinically Grounded Gaze Biomarkers
Measures 16 objective digital biomarkers validated across peer-reviewed clinical research (including studies published in JAMA, NEJM AI, and eLife):
- Social Visual Preference (salience of human social interaction vs. dynamic geometric shapes)
- First Eye Fixation Latency (speed of initial attention orientation to human eyes)
- Eye-to-Mouth Dwell Distribution (balance of gaze across facial features)
- Synchronized Affect & Smile Contagion (shared enjoyment and smile response latency)
- Visual Scanning Stability & Dispersion

• Gentle 5-Point Bubble Calibration
Calibrate within seconds using a delightful bubble-popping mini-game. Designed specifically to capture a toddler's natural curiosity without rigid head restraints.

• Standardized M-CHAT-R/F Questionnaire
Complete the American Academy of Pediatrics recommended 20-item Modified Checklist for Autism in Toddlers (M-CHAT-R/F) directly within the app, coded with official LOINC clinical identifiers (LOINC 80628-1).

• Comprehensive Pediatrician PDF Reports
Generate publication-grade clinical summary reports with a single tap. Reports feature benchmark comparisons against age-matched normative distributions (12–18m, 18–24m, 24–36m, 36–48m), detailed biomarker breakdowns, and actionable clinical talking points for your developmental pediatrician or early intervention team.

• CareKit Developmental Diary
Track daily routines, sensory sensitivities, communication milestones, and sleep patterns with Apple CareKit integration.

• 100% Video-Free Medical Privacy-by-Design
We believe pediatric privacy is sacred:
- ZERO raw camera frames, facial images, or video clips are ever saved to device storage or uploaded to the cloud.
- Eye-gaze vectors are processed strictly in volatile device memory and immediately discarded.
- Only anonymized numerical time-series and biomarker summary scores are preserved on your device.

REGULATORY & MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:
EarlyGaze is a clinical decision-support and developmental tracking aid designed to provide objective behavioral observations for parents and healthcare professionals. EarlyGaze is not a standalone diagnostic medical device and does not diagnose Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) or Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD). Formal diagnostic evaluation must always be performed by a qualified physician or developmental specialist.
