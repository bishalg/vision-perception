Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing EarlyGaze. Below is a guide on how to evaluate the application:

1. HARDWARE & PERMISSIONS:
- The app requires front-facing TrueDepth camera access (ARKit Face Tracking) to compute gaze direction and measure social visual engagement.
- Medical Privacy-by-Design: No video feeds, facial photos, or AR mesh streams are ever recorded, persisted to storage, or transmitted off-device (Guideline 5.1.1 & 5.1.2 compliant). The camera data is solely processed in volatile RAM in real time.

2. STEP-BY-STEP REVIEW GUIDE:
Step 1: Onboarding
- Launch the application and progress through the child profile setup.
- Accept the Camera and Motion permissions when prompted.
- Review and tap "Accept & Continue" on the Research/Consent screen.

Step 2: Bubble Calibration
- Position the device in front of your face (approx 40-50cm).
- Tap the 5 popping bubbles on screen to establish calibrated spatial eye geometry.

Step 3: Gaze Screening Session
- Select a screening battery (e.g. Standard 30s Battery).
- Watch the stimulus video. The TrueDepth sensor will track eye fixations, measuring attention between social and geometric cues.

Step 4: View Results & Biomarkers
- Once playback finishes, inspect the 16 objective digital biomarkers (Social Preference Score, First Eye Latency, Eye Dwell Ratio, Heatmap).

Step 5: M-CHAT-R Questionnaire
- Tap the "Survey" tab or card to inspect the standardized 20-item M-CHAT-R/F screener (LOINC 80628-1).

Step 6: Clinical PDF Export
- Tap "Export Doctor Report" on any session to preview the multi-page clinical PDF report designed for developmental pediatricians.

3. MEDICAL DISCLAIMERS:
- EarlyGaze displays prominent medical disclaimers on onboarding, within the results dashboard, and on all exported clinical PDF reports (Guideline 1.4.1 compliant). It is explicitly designated as a clinical decision-support and developmental monitoring aid, not a standalone diagnostic instrument.
