EarlyGaze is an at-home developmental visual attention observation and screening tool designed for parents and pediatricians to track social engagement and focus patterns in children aged 12–60 months.

Using advanced front-facing eye tracking and computer vision, EarlyGaze captures objective visual engagement markers during standardized visual stimuli (social versus geometric preference, joint attention, and face scanning tasks). Digital biomarkers are computed 100% on-device — including gaze fixation duration, saccade frequency, dwell ratio, and attention shifts.

KEY SCREENING & MONITORING FEATURES:
• 100% Video-Free Privacy: Camera video streams are analyzed strictly in temporary device memory. No photos or video recordings of your child are ever stored or transmitted.
• Objective Visual Attention Metrics: Scientifically grounded visual preference paradigms measuring social response latency and gaze stability.
• Standardized M-CHAT-R/F Integration: Integrated 20-item Modified Checklist for Autism in Toddlers with instant scoring and follow-up guidance.
• Comprehensive Pediatric PDF Reports: Generate clear, objective visual engagement summaries to share directly with your child's pediatrician or developmental specialist.
• Longitudinal Progress Tracking: Monitor attention milestones, developmental shifts, and sensory routines over time.

SCIENTIFIC FOUNDATION:
EarlyGaze visual engagement paradigms draw on published research in pediatric visual attention, including the GeoPref paradigm and digital biomarker studies from leading neurodevelopmental laboratories.

IMPORTANT MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:
EarlyGaze is an observational screening aid and developmental monitoring reference for parents and caregivers. It is NOT a diagnostic medical device, does NOT diagnose Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), ADHD, or any medical condition, and does NOT replace comprehensive evaluation by a licensed pediatrician or child psychologist. Always consult a qualified medical professional regarding developmental concerns.
