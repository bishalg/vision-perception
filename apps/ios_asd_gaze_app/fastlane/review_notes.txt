Thank you for reviewing EarlyGaze.

What the app is
EarlyGaze is an observation capture tool used with a clinic. During a short session the child watches child-friendly clips while the front TrueDepth camera notes where they look and how their face moves. The session is stored on the device, or delivered to the clinic's console once the clinic has enrolled the device. The app shows no score, result or interpretation on the device; only the clinic reviews sessions, on its own platform. This is not a diagnostic tool. It does not measure, screen for or diagnose any condition.

Reviewing without a clinic
No account or login is needed. The build you are reviewing is local-only: no clinic server is configured, so "Clinic delivery" has nothing to send to and every session stays on the device. The enrolment code field appears only after a clinic configuration ships; you will not see it in this build.

Suggested path
1. First launch: the consent flow (guardian name and relationship; any name works) and a short child profile.
2. Capture tab: "Before you begin" explains what is recorded, then the camera primer, then the session. A camera check and calibration run first. Observation sessions need a device with Face ID (TrueDepth); on other devices the Capture tab explains this and Settings still work.
3. After the session: a recording-quality summary only (usable samples, where the session is stored). No result is shown.
4. Sessions tab: the list of sessions on the device, with the same recording-quality information.
5. Settings, Privacy & Your Data: every optional feature with its switch (all off by default), the iOS permission status, the signed consent record, and Delete all data.

Privacy
Camera frames are processed in memory and never saved or sent. Session data is encrypted on the device and excluded from backups. The app has no analytics, ads or tracking, and the privacy manifest declares no collected data because this build sends nothing off the device.

Health data
The app does not use HealthKit. The App Store build carries no HealthKit entitlement and never reads or writes Health data.

Declared Age Range
The app can ask iOS for the declared age range of the signed-in account as an optional check; it is off by default and the app works without it.
