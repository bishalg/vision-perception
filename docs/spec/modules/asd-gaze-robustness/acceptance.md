# ASD Gaze Tracking Robustness - Acceptance

Scenarios an owner or reviewer can run. Each cites the spec ids it proves and the work
package that makes it pass. Rust commands assume `--manifest-path rust/Cargo.toml` and
`CARGO_TARGET_DIR=/private/tmp/asd-rust`.

| # | Scenario | Proves | WP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | On a device, start the Express battery, cover the camera with a hand for about 3 s during a clip, finish. The session's `validFrameRatio` is lower than in an identical session without the cover, and the frame export contains frames with `valid: false` for the covered period. The wizard offers no "use saved calibration" option; on a calibration where one balloon is never looked at, the target repeats once and then calibration fails visibly instead of finishing. Rotating the device during capture does not rotate the screen. | TRK-1, CAL-1, CAL-3, GEO-3 | G.0 |
