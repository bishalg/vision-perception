## 1. Executive Summary & Biological Grounding

While behavioral eye tracking has established that many autistic toddlers show diminished attention to social interactions, the **underlying neurofunctional mechanism** remained elusive.

This seminal paper directly bridges **behavioral digital phenotyping (the GeoPref eye-tracking test)** with **in vivo human brain functional architecture (natural-sleep resting-state fMRI)** in a large cohort of toddlers aged 12 to 48 months.

The authors demonstrate that autistic toddlers who exhibit a pronounced preference for viewing repetitive geometric animations ($\ge 69\%$ fixation time on dynamic geometric patterns vs social interactions) possess a distinct biological phenotype characterized by **functional hypoconnectivity between the Default Mode Network (DMN) and the Visual Association Cortex**.

## 3. The GeoPref Test: Mechanics & Clinical Utility

### 3.1 Stimulus Design
- A standardized 60-second video split symmetrically into two simultaneous streams:
  - **Social Scene**: High-energy, colorful videos of toddlers engaging in biological motion (dancing, yoga, exercises).
  - **Dynamic Geometric Scene**: Non-biological, abstract geometric patterns (kaleidoscope motion, moving geometric shapes).
- Sound tracks are balanced to avoid spatial auditory bias; audio is centered and rhythmic.

### 3.2 The 69% Threshold Invariant
- Seminal work by Karen Pierce (2011, 2016, 2022) established that neurotypical toddlers spend $>70\%$ of their viewing time inspecting the social side.
- Autistic toddlers exhibit a bimodal distribution:
  - Approximately **$20\%\text{–}25\%$** of autistic toddlers show an extreme preference for the geometric patterns ($\ge 69\%$ fixation time).
  - **Diagnostic Specificity**: The $\ge 69\%$ threshold achieves **$98\%\text{–}99\%$ positive predictive specificity for ASD** (almost zero neurotypical toddlers or non-ASD developmentally delayed toddlers exceed $69\%$ geometric preference).
- **Prognostic Value**: Toddlers in the ASD-GeoPref subtype exhibit significantly lower expressive language scores, more severe social affect impairments, and higher ADOS scores than autistic peers who prefer social stimuli.

## 4. Neuroimaging Findings: DMN-Visual Hypoconnectivity

### 4.1 The Default Mode Network (DMN) in Early Social Development
The DMN (comprising medial prefrontal cortex [mPFC], posterior cingulate cortex [PCC] / precuneus, and bilateral angular gyrus) constitutes the neural core of self-referential cognition, mentalizing, and social-emotional processing.

### 4.2 Hypoconnectivity Findings
1. **DMN $\leftrightarrow$ Visual Association Cortex**:
   - The ASD-GeoPref subtype demonstrated statistically robust **hypoconnectivity between the DMN and visual association areas (lateral occipital cortex / extrastriate visual areas)**.
   - Contrast: ASD-Social toddlers, typically developing toddlers, and developmentally delayed controls all exhibited preserved functional connectivity between these networks.
2. **Biological Mechanism**:
   - In neurotypical development, the social brain network dynamically modulates visual sensory gating—making human faces and biological motion inherently salient and rewarding.
   - In the ASD-GeoPref subtype, uncoupling between the DMN and visual cortex removes top-down social salience modulation, allowing lower-level sensory features (high-contrast edges, periodic motion, rotational symmetry) to dominate gaze allocation.
