## Part B. Recipient promises (identified data)
1. Use and disclose the data only for the purpose in A.1, and not for marketing, sale, or training of models, unless the guardian separately consents.
2. Apply administrative, physical and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity, consistent with the HIPAA Security Rule where it applies to the Recipient, and in any case: encrypted storage, restricted access, a unique login per person, no shared mailboxes.
3. Download via the share link within its lifetime; do not forward the link or the passcode. Store the file only in the Recipient's secured system.
4. Not re-identify, contact the child or family, or link the data to other data, except through the Clinic.
5. Not disclose to any other person except staff who need it, and subcontractors bound in writing by terms at least as strict. Report onward disclosure to the Clinic.
6. **Incident notice:** notify the Clinic's privacy contact **within 72 hours** [OWNER DECISION: O-11, recommended default] of discovering any use or disclosure not allowed here, loss, or unauthorised access, with what, whose, when, and what was done.
7. Make records of disclosures available to the Clinic within 10 business days of request, to support the family's access.
8. **Return or destroy** all copies and backups within [30] days of the end of the purpose or on request, and confirm in writing. If destruction is not feasible, extend these promises and restrict use.
9. Cooperate with the Clinic on a family's request to see, correct or delete data, and with the Clinic's audit questions.
10. Permit the Clinic to verify compliance by written questionnaire; no on-site audit unless a serious incident.

## Part C. De-identified data (all cases)
The Recipient will not attempt to identify any person, will not share the data beyond the named users, will cite no individual, and will notify the Clinic if it believes identification has become possible. Data is watermarked and may be traced to this agreement.

## Part D. Clinic promises
The Clinic obtained the guardian's consent, shares the minimum needed for the purpose, sends the passcode by a channel other than the link, can revoke the link, keeps a disclosure record, and will tell the Recipient promptly if the guardian withdraws consent or asks for erasure so the Recipient can act within the law.
