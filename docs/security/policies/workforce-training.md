# Workforce training and sanctions policy

> **DRAFT. Requires legal review before use.** WP-E.8, 2026-11-02. Written for a small clinic with no IT staff. It does not claim that the product or the clinic is HIPAA compliant or certified. Items marked [OWNER DECISION: O-x] are recommended defaults awaiting the owner.

Maps to: 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(C), (a)(3), (a)(5); Privacy Act: duty of confidentiality.

## Who must be trained
Everyone with a console login, a clinic tablet or access to the server room: clinicians, nurses, front desk, volunteers, cleaners who enter the room, and any outside helper. The PSO (`access-control.md`) runs it.

## When
- Before the first sign-in (joiners).
- Every year (refresher, 30 minutes).
- After any incident that shows a gap, and after a major version upgrade that changes screens.

## Content checklist (30 to 45 minutes, in Nepali or the person's language)
- [ ] What the data is: children's health observations; why it is sensitive; the clinic's duty to families.
- [ ] One person, one login; two-step sign-in; passwords; signing out.
- [ ] Tablets: passcode, never leave unattended, never photograph the screen with a personal phone, lost device means tell the PSO at once (`device-and-media.md`).
- [ ] Only look at what you need. Reads are logged.
- [ ] No sending records by e-mail, chat apps or personal drives. Use the share-a-case feature with the signed consent only.
- [ ] Recognise a phishing message or a call asking for a password; the vendor never asks for passwords.
- [ ] How to report an incident, and that reporting in good faith is never punished (`incident-response-and-breach.md`).
- [ ] Consent: what the family signed; what "share with a doctor abroad" means; never share without the signed form.
- [ ] Emergency procedure and where the key card is kept (only the owner and the named second person need to know the place).
- [ ] Paper: printed summaries and consent forms are records too; lock them away.

## Confidentiality pledge (sign once, keep in the staff file)
"I will keep every child's and family's information confidential, use it only for my work, look only at what I need, never share it outside the clinic except through the clinic's approved process, and tell the PSO at once about any loss or misuse. I understand this duty continues after I leave." Name, signature, date: ______________

## Sanctions
Applied fairly and in proportion; written down by the PSO.
1. **Careless** (left a screen open, weak password): retraining and a note on file.
2. **Rule-breaking without harm** (shared a login, looked at a record without need): written warning, access reviewed, retraining.
3. **Deliberate misuse or disclosure** (sold, posted or sent records, repeated snooping): access removed at once; employment or contract action; report to the family and the authority where the law requires [OWNER DECISION: O-11]; possible legal action.
